Brute-force protection

The sign-in form is limited on two levels at once:

  • Per address: 10 attempts a minute, so one host cannot spray guesses.
  • Per account: after 5 consecutive failures the account locks, starting at 15 seconds and doubling with every further failure up to 15 minutes.

A successful sign-in clears the failure history. Counters live in memory, so restarting the controller resets them.
